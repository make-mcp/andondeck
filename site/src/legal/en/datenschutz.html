Hosting and server log data

The website is hosted by ALL-INKL.COM – Neue Medien Münnich, Hauptstraße 68, 02742 Friedersdorf, Germany. When you visit it, the web server automatically records information your browser transmits: IP address, date and time, requested page, referrer, browser and operating system. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure operation). Log files are kept as long as the hoster specifies and then deleted or anonymised. A data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR is in place with the hoster.

Encryption

The website is delivered exclusively over an encrypted HTTPS connection (TLS).

Language setting in your browser

When you choose a language or close the language hint, this choice is stored in your browser's local storage (localStorage) so the site appears in the same language on your next visit. It stays on your device, is not sent to the server and can be deleted at any time in your browser settings. Storing it is strictly necessary for the function you requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

Contact by email

If you email me, I process your details to handle your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them as soon as they are no longer needed and no statutory retention obligations apply.

External links

Links to GitHub, Elgato Marketplace and other services are plain links. Data is only transmitted to the respective provider when you click such a link; their privacy policy then applies.

The Andon Deck software

Andon Deck runs entirely on your computer and talks to Claude Code over a local port. There is no central service and no usage data is sent to me. It reads Claude Code's local transcripts only to show your usage history on the deck and never uploads them.
